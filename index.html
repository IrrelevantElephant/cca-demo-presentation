The essentials / Incident response

What is MTTR?

Mean time to recovery
(or repair).

A measure of how long it takes to restore service after an incident is detected.

Why it matters

MTTR measures how effectively we respond when things go wrong.

Recovery matters
Reducing MTTR with AI AgentsMeasure recovery
MTTR has several common expansions, including mean time to recovery and mean time to repair. For this talk, use it as the time from incident detection to restored service. Be explicit about the chosen start and end points when measuring it.

Prevention and response / Both matter

Shift left—and be ready.

01 / Prevent

Shift-left development

Good testing and QA catch defects earlier and reduce risk.

  • Build quality into delivery
  • Find issues before production
02 / Recover

Expect incidents

Testing cannot eliminate every production failure. Plan to respond and restore service.

  • Make incidents observable
  • Improve recovery time (MTTR)
Reducing MTTR with AI AgentsPrevent + recover
This is not an argument against shift-left. Testing and QA are essential, but complex systems still fail in production. MTTR is one way to assess how well we handle those failures.
